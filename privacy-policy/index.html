Summary

Gem Catch is a single-player arcade game that can be played offline, with an optional ad-supported free tier and a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads.

  • We do not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information.
  • Gem Catch does not require an account or sign-in.
  • High scores, settings, and game progress are stored locally on your device.
  • Google AdMob may process device and advertising data when ads are enabled.
  • Apple and Google process purchases through their respective app stores.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Advertising

Gem Catch uses Google AdMob to show an interstitial ad after a game over unless you have purchased Remove Ads. AdMob may process information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

Google's handling of that information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Purchasing Remove Ads disables the ads integration for future gameplay according to the current game configuration.

In-app purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play using the payment method connected to your store account. We do not receive, view, or store your payment card details. The game receives only the purchase status needed to unlock the ad-free experience on your device.

Information stored on your device

Gem Catch uses Unity PlayerPrefs, backed by the platform's local storage, to save:

  • Your high score.
  • Your daily-challenge progress and current daily best score.
  • Audio preferences and other gameplay settings.
  • Whether Remove Ads has been purchased.

This game data remains on your device. You can clear it by uninstalling Gem Catch or clearing the app's data in your device settings. If you reinstall, use the in-game Restore Purchases option to recover an eligible store purchase.

Permissions

Gem Catch requests only the permissions required by the Unity engine, Google AdMob, and in-app purchasing, such as internet access for ads and store communication. The game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or general file storage.

If a future update needs a new permission, this policy will be updated and the game will request permission at runtime where required.

Children's privacy

Gem Catch is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 in the United States or under 16 in the European Economic Area. If the intended audience changes, advertising settings and this policy must be reviewed before that change is released.

Crash and diagnostic data

Google Play and the Android operating system may automatically collect crash reports and basic device information when an app crashes. This information is collected by Google and governed by Google's privacy policy. Gem Catch does not enable Unity Cloud Diagnostics or another third-party crash-reporting service in the current documented configuration.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically for updates.
